Why Orarpix?

Orarpix is named after the three cats who are around for the making of every game.

It takes the first syllable of each name: Or + Ar + Pix. In the logo, each cat sits above its syllable.

  • Orion

    Orange tabby, curious and always the first to arrive.

  • Arwen

    Brown tabby, calm and observant.

  • Pixie

    Black and white, with a tuxedo coat.
